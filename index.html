FAQ

What do you make?

Graphics and media for local businesses: social media posts, menu designs, ad creatives, and short motion graphics for Reels, Stories, and ads.

Do you build websites or do photo shoots?

No. We don't build websites and we don't do photo shoots. We work with the photos, logos, and brand materials you already have, and we can make AI-assisted visuals when you don't have photos.

Is it really 48 hours?

Yes. Once we have your request and materials, we deliver the first version within 48 hours. Add 24-hour rush for 30% more.

How does the free sample work?

Send us one existing post, flyer, or menu and we'll refresh it for free so you can see the quality before you pay anything. No obligation.

What does "AI-assisted" mean?

We use AI tools to move faster and explore ideas. A human designer reviews and finishes every piece so it stays on-brand and accurate.

Where are you located?

Valley Stream / North Woodmere in Nassau County. We work with businesses across Long Island, and everything can be handled remotely.

How many revisions do I get?

One round of revisions comes with every one-time package. Always Fresh clients get ongoing tweaks every month.

How do I pay?

We'll send a simple invoice after you approve your quote. One-time packages are paid on delivery approval, and Always Fresh is billed monthly.
